Hello everyone,

I am presenting my SWYNEX internship Task 1 project, titled
"Student Feedback Sentiment Classifier."

The practical AI problem I selected is student feedback classification.

Colleges receive many feedback comments, and manually categorizing them can take time. My project uses Natural Language Processing and machine learning to classify feedback as Positive, Negative, or Neutral.

For the dataset, I created a small sample CSV containing student feedback and sentiment labels. The dataset does not contain personal information.

The target users are college faculty and academic coordinators.

For the AI approach, I used TF-IDF to convert text into numerical features and Logistic Regression for classification.

I divided the dataset into training and testing data. I evaluated the model using accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score.

The success criteria are that the model should classify new feedback correctly and achieve approximately 80 percent or higher accuracy on a suitable test split.
